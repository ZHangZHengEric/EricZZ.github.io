## 隐私 / Privacy

我重视访问者的隐私。未经你的明确同意，我不会向他人分享你的电子邮件地址或其他个人信息。

I respect the privacy of visitors. I will not share your email address or other personal information without your explicit consent.

## 日志 / Logs

网站托管服务可能会记录 IP 地址、浏览器类型、访问时间、来源页面和基础访问数据，用于安全防护与运行分析。这些信息不会由我用于识别特定个人。

The hosting provider may record IP addresses, browser types, access times, referring pages, and basic traffic data for security and operational analysis. I do not use this information to identify individual visitors.

## Cookie 与第三方服务 / Cookies & Third-party Services

本网站自身不使用广告 Cookie。外部链接或嵌入的第三方服务可能适用各自的隐私政策；你也可以通过浏览器设置限制 Cookie。

This site does not use advertising cookies. External links or embedded third-party services may be governed by their own privacy policies, and cookies can be limited through your browser settings.
